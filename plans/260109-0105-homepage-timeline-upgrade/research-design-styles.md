# Research Report: Design Styles & Trends for Company Memory Timeline (2025-2026)

## Executive Summary
This report outlines the visual strategy for upgrading the Company Memory Timeline, moving from standard glassmorphism to a more structured, modular, and "human-centric" design. The goal is to blend Teky's tech-forward brand with the emotional resonance of a memory platform.

## 1. Design Style Trends (2025-2026)
- **Bento Grids 2.0 (Active Grids):** Beyond static tiles. Use modular, asymmetrical grids where tiles are interactive (expanding on hover, playing video previews).
- **Glassmorphism 2.0:** Shifting from heavy blurs to "Subtle Layering." High-transparency surfaces with thin, luminous borders (1px) to create depth without clutter.
- **Warm Minimalism:** Combining clean UI with organic shapes and soft shadows to avoid a "sterile" corporate feel.
- **Spatial UI Elements:** Subtle 3D depth and micro-animations (buttons that "depress," cards that tilt based on device orientation).

## 2. Color Palette & Psychology (Memory & Nostalgia)
Focus on "Digital Calm" and "Stability."

| Role | Color Name | HSL Value | Psychology |

## 3. Typography Recommendations (Vietnamese Support)
Primary focus on readability for Vietnamese diacritics.

- **Primary Heading: Be Vietnam Pro**
  - *Weights:* 700 (Bold), 500 (Medium)
  - *Sizes:* Desktop 48px, Mobile 32px
  - *Why:* Specifically optimized for Vietnamese letterforms.
- **Body & UI: Inter**
  - *Weights:* 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-bold)
  - *Sizes:* 16px (Body), 14px (Meta/Date)
  - *Why:* Exceptional legibility at small sizes and high x-height.

## 4. Card Design & Timeline Patterns
- **Variable Aspect Ratio Cards:** Mix 4:5 (portrait) for photos and 16:9 for videos within a Bento Grid.
- **Floating Timelines:** A vertical line that is not solid but made of "Pulse Points" (micro-animated dots) that glow when the user scrolls past.
- **Contextual Cards:** Cards that reveal "Wish Text" or "Comments" only on interaction to keep the initial view clean.

## 5. Visual Hierarchy Recommendations
1. **The Hero:** Large, animated gradient background with "Active" theme elements (e.g., falling petals).
2. **The Bento Grid:** High-density photo gallery below the hero.
3. **The Interaction:** 0.2s ease-out transitions for all hover states.
